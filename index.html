Electronics Foundations & Introduction to C++

D1D15D30

Phase 1Hardware & Electronics FoundationsDays 1–14

Days 1–3

Core Electronic Components & Multimeter Practice

  • Learn active vs. passive components (Resistors, Capacitors, Diodes, Transistors, Switches).
  • Use a digital multimeter to measure voltage drops, resistance, and continuity testing on a breadboard.
Days 4–6

Hardware Wiring & Schematic Literacy

  • Understand breadboard schematics, power rails, common grounds, pull-up/pull-down resistors, and decoupling capacitors.
  • Wire basic circuits (LEDs with current-limiting resistors, push-button switches, and relay modules).
Days 7–10

Soldering & Connectors

  • Practice with through-hole soldering irons (350°C–400°C), lead-free solder, flux, heat shrink tubing, and wire strippers.
  • Solder header pins onto breakout boards and build custom wire harnesses.
Days 11–14

Hardware Identification & Sensor Overview

  • Overview of sensors (Ultrasonic HC-SR04, IR obstacle sensors, IMUs, rotary encoders) and actuators (DC motors, steppers, servos, L298N drivers).

Phase 2Microcontroller & Arduino ProgrammingDays 15–30

Days 15–30

Arduino IDE Setup & C++ Introduction

  • Install the Arduino IDE, manage libraries, and learn basic C++ syntax (variables, data types, functions, and control structures).
  • Write your first microcontroller scripts to read digital/analog pins and manage serial monitor debugging.
